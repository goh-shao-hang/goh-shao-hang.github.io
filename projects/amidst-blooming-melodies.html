Narrative Design

Narrative Background

You play as Flos against the Giant Voiceless — a bunny-shaped, single-eyed "Corrupted Collective" boss — across a three-phase fight with a full transition framework and a sing-to-recover QTE mechanic. The project runs a between-subjects study comparing an audio/narrative-ON build against an OFF build, with mechanical difficulty held constant across both conditions so the comparison isolates framing, not skill.
