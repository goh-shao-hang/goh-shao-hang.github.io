Curse Breaker: Awakening

Trailer

Project Overview

Winning entry of the Best Final Year Project Award at Asia Pacific University. A roguelike dungeon-crawler with procedural dungeon generation, focused on movement and environmental interaction mechanics.

Design Breakdown

Core Mechanics

Curse Breaker plays like a boomer shooter with fast movement, but mostly melee. The player uses wall running and a charged lunge attack to move through procedurally generated rooms, defeat enemies, collect gold and find the boss. The lunge's damage and travel distance scale with charge time, making it both the main attack and a key way to get around. Sprinting and wall running spend stamina, which the player has to manage.

Procedural Dungeon Generation

The goal is to create a dungeon generation algorithm that is both modular and highly customisable: new rules or modules can be added without touching the generator itself.

  • Step 1: Abstract Data Generation

    The dungeon layout is generated through an abstract grid-based data generator based on predetermined parameters such as room count and grid size.

  • Step 2: Room IDs and Custom Rules

    The data is then processed, with custom IDs applied to them or special conditions, enforcing certain rules, such as marking a start and end room, room types that have to be next to another room type and more.

  • Step 3: Modules

    Modules are then created to interpret the data for various gameplay purposes.

    The final video shows both the playable in-game rooms generated using the data by the Room Spawner module, and the map interpreted from the same data by a Map Manager module which also self-updates to reveal rooms as player discovers them.

The whole system strictly abides by the SOLID programming principles.

Environmental Interaction

The game provides various means for the player to interact with what they see and use them in combat.

Players can pick and throw anything with a yellow outline, including crates, weapons dropped by enemies, and even stunned enemies. Enemies can be thrown at each other to deal damage or into environmental hazards.

During playtests, players adapted various creative strategies in combat, from throwing enemies into lava for instant-kill to saving up dropped weapons during a boss fight to throw at the boss for high burst-damage.

Boss Design Overview

The Feuerhexe (fire witch) boss has two phases, with abilities designed to keep the player moving around the arena instead of sticking close to her. Phase 2 upgrades them (five fireballs instead of three, three summons instead of two) and adds a Blast that destroys arena tiles.

When her health is depleted, she becomes undamageable and smashes the floor causing everything to drop into a lava-surrounded arena. Then, phase 2 begins and she regains all her health. It is designed as a deliberate surprise for first-time players. Reactions ranged from “wait, there's more?” to “Oh damn, I'm not going to beat this”.

The arena shrinks as the boss performs her blast attack, which breaks the tile she stands on. However, friendly fire applies and the lava kills enemies instantly. The fallen pillars can now also be pushed around (to block projectiles, enemies, or pushed into lava to create standable ground).

The key intent of all these designs is to ensure strategies come not only from abilities integrated into the player, but also the environment.

The boss is built on an earlier version of the game-object-based ability system from Amidst Blooming Melodies.
